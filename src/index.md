I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Electrical Engineering department at Stanford University, where I'm advised by [Ram Rajagopal](https://profiles.stanford.edu/ram-rajagopal). I am fortunate to be supported by the [Stanford Graduate Fellowship](https://vpge.stanford.edu/fellowships-funding/sgf) as a Burt and Deedee McMurtry Fellow.

I work on large-scale optimization, learning, and control to make energy and computing infrastructure more efficient and reliable. I am particularly interested in the intersection of optimization and machine learning for developing robust and scalable algorithms. 

Previously, I worked as an electrical engineer at Apple and a data scientist at Gridmatic. I earned my B.S. in EECS at UC Berkeley and my M.S. in electrical and computer engineering at UCLA.
